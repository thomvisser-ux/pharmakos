# P6 task card A3

**Open this file only when the scratch watch has started.** The rule it serves is section 5.3's
closing rule (`docs/design/s3-plan.md`); the record is `docs/demo/p6c-record.md`.

Use the map's click menu. The places are yours to choose. Each numbered line is one route step, added
to the empty playbook that "New" put on screen.

1. Place a beacon on ground inside your sphere ("Place beacon").
2. Walk to a spot of ground beside that beacon ("Go here").
3. Visit your core and set its priority **high** ("Visit & change").
4. Walk to a spot of ground near your core.
5. Walk to your core.
6. Place a beacon on another spot of ground inside your sphere.
7. Walk to another spot of ground near your core.
8. Click a heat vent. The menu offers two rows, "Place beacon covering this vent" and then "Place
   beacon covering the nearest vent you can cover": take the second. If you take the first instead,
   say so. If the menu says "Go here"
   and "Place beacon", you missed the vent, so press Escape and click closer to its middle.
9. Walk to your core.
10. Visit your core and set its priority **normal**.

Then press "Submit".

If a step is refused, read the refusal, put it right, and say so: the time runs on.
