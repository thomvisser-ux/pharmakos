# P6 task card A

**Open this file only when the scratch watch has started.** The run sheet is
`docs/demo/p6-run-sheet.md`.

Use the map's click menu. The places are yours to choose. Each numbered line is one route step, added
after the two steps the template gave you.

The map opens zoomed out, with your core at the edge of the panel. Pan it clear with D or the arrows
and wheel in over it before your first click. Camera moves are inside the time.

1. Walk to a spot of ground near your core ("Go here").
2. Visit your core and set its priority **high** ("Visit & change").
3. Place a beacon on ground inside your sphere ("Place beacon").
4. Walk to a spot of ground beside that beacon.
5. Alt-click a heat vent. The menu says "Place beacon covering the nearest vent you can cover": take
   it. If it says "Go here", you missed, so press Escape and click closer to the vent's middle. If it
   says "Place beacon covering this vent", take that and say so.
6. Walk to your core.
7. Visit your core and set its priority **normal**.
8. Walk to another spot of ground near your core.
9. Place a beacon on another spot of ground inside your sphere.
10. Walk to your core.

Then press "Submit".
