# P6b task card A2

**Open this file only when P6b's scratch watch has started.** The run sheet is
`docs/demo/p6-run-sheet.md`.

Card A's ten kinds of step, in a different order. Use the map's click menu. The places are yours
to choose. Each numbered line is one route step, added after the two steps the template gave you.

The map opens on your commander, at your core. Your core's sphere reaches about 24 voxels from it.

1. Visit your core and set its priority **high** (click your core, then "Visit & change", then
   "Set priority high").
2. Click the heat vent nearest your core, about 40 voxels away; the panel heads the menu "Heat
   vent (175, 66)". Take "Place beacon covering the nearest vent you can cover". If you take
   "Place beacon covering this vent" instead, say so.
3. Walk to your core (click your core, then "Go here").
4. Place a beacon on ground inside your sphere ("Place beacon").
5. Walk to a spot of ground beside that beacon ("Go here").
6. Walk to another spot of ground near your core.
7. Place a beacon on another spot of ground inside your sphere.
8. Walk to your core.
9. Visit your core and set its priority **normal**.
10. Walk to a spot of ground near your core.

Then press "Submit".
