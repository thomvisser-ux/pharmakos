# P6b task card A2

**Open this file only when P6b's scratch watch has started.** The run sheet is
`docs/demo/p6-run-sheet.md`.

Use the map's click menu. The places are yours to choose. Each numbered line is one route step, added
after the two steps the template gave you.

1. Visit your core and set its priority **high** ("Visit & change").
2. Click a heat vent. The menu offers two rows: take "Place beacon covering the nearest vent you can
   cover". If you take "Place beacon covering this vent" instead, say so. If the menu says "Go here"
   and "Place beacon", you missed the vent, so press Escape and click closer to its middle.
3. Walk to your core.
4. Place a beacon on ground inside your sphere ("Place beacon").
5. Walk to a spot of ground beside that beacon ("Go here").
6. Walk to another spot of ground near your core.
7. Place a beacon on another spot of ground inside your sphere.
8. Walk to your core.
9. Visit your core and set its priority **normal**.
10. Walk to a spot of ground near your core.

Then press "Submit".

If a step is refused, read the refusal, put it right, and say so: the time runs on.
