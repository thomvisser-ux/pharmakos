Expand & Mine
Written from a built-in template.
Place a Mine beacon covering an ore seam you do not cover yet, and walk home while it digs.

Route
  1. place_mine: place a beacon at a site covering the nearest scrap seam you do not cover yet (nearest by travel, read when the step starts), tagged "mine", on a Mine mandate, at normal Quartermaster priority (16.5 s on site).
    Give up after 2:00.
    On failure, move on to the next step.
  2. back_home: walk to the safest own beacon.

Rules
  1. "flee", when the commander is at or below 40% HP and the commander took damage in the last 3 s:
    1. flee_to_safety: walk to the safest own beacon.
    2. flee_wait: hold for 8 s.
    Then carry on with the route.
    Fires at most 3 times, and no sooner than every 30 s.

If the commander dies
  On respawn, carry on from the step the route was on.
  After 2 deaths the fallback takes over.

Fallback
  Hold at the safest own beacon.

Budget
  5 of 128 size units.
  16.5 s standing at a beacon along the route.
