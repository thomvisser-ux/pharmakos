Safe playbook
Written from a built-in template.
Move to the safest beacon, raise the priority of any beacon about to brown out, then stay with the safest beacon.

Route
  1. to_safety: walk to the safest own beacon, avoiding known threats.
  2. deepen_core: interface with beacon "b_00" and change its mandate settings (3.5 s on site).
    On failure, move on to the next step.

Rules
  1. "flee", when the commander is at or below 40% HP and the commander took damage in the last 3 s:
    1. flee_to_safety: walk to the safest own beacon.
    2. flee_wait: hold for 8 s.
    Then carry on with the route.
    Fires at most 3 times, and no sooner than every 30 s.

If the commander dies
  On respawn, carry on from the step the route was on.

Fallback
  Stay with the safest own beacon.

Budget
  5 of 128 size units.
  3.5 s standing at a beacon along the route.
