Expand & Mine east
Written by hand.

Route
  1. to_site: walk to voxel 96, 11, 62, avoiding known threats.
    Give up after 2:00.
  2. place_east: place a beacon at voxel 96, 11, 62, tagged "east", on a Mine mandate, at normal Quartermaster priority (16.5 s on site).
  3. back_home: walk to beacon "b_01".

Rules
  1. "flee", when the commander is at or below 40% HP and the commander took damage in the last 3 s:
    1. f1: walk to the safest own beacon.
    2. f2: hold for 8 s.
    Then carry on with the route.
    Fires at most 3 times, and no sooner than every 30 s.

If the commander dies
  On respawn, carry on from the step the route was on.
  After 2 deaths the fallback takes over.

Fallback
  Hold at the safest own beacon.

Budget
  6 of 128 size units.
  16.5 s standing at a beacon along the route.
